# 460 Trip Planner — Quick Start

*You've been invited to help plan and share part of a trip. Here's the 2-minute version — the full User Guide lives inside the app under **Help**.*

## What it is

A private, invite-only travel planner and journal — one place where everyone on a
trip sees the same plan, day by day, on their phone or laptop. It isn't an
app-store app: you open it in your browser and add it to your home screen so it
feels like one. No fees, no adverts, and only people we invite can use it.

**Open it at → https://460planner.pwkconsulting.org**

## Getting in (about 5 minutes)

1. **Create your account.** Open the link above, tap **Register**, and sign up
   with your own email. Each person needs their own account — installing the app
   alone isn't enough; the *account* is what unlocks everything.
2. **Add it to your home screen** so it opens like an app:
   - **iPhone / iPad (Safari):** Share → **Add to Home Screen**.
   - **Android (Chrome):** menu (⋮) → **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
3. **Travelling as a couple or family?** In **Settings → Account → Household**,
   link the other person, so you're added to trips together and *both* of you see
   the shared days. Invites show up on the in-app **notification bell** (top of
   the screen) — not by email.

## Joining the shared trip

4. Peter sends you a **segment invite link**. Open it and choose **"Attach to my
   trip"** (or "Create a new trip from it" if you haven't started one). The shared
   days now appear in *your* trip and stay in sync with everyone else's.

## What's shared — and what stays yours

| Shared with the group | Stays private to you |
|---|---|
| The itinerary for the shared days — places, their order, times and notes | Your own flights, hotels and their booking documents |
| The day titles | Your own journal entries and photos |

You plan the shared days *together*; your bookings, journal and photos stay yours.
